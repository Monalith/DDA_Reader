# BMW reference fit (2026-10-11)

Branch `feature/bmw-fit`. Owner's requirement: *first model the BMW rider from the speed and line in
every corner; once that model fits in time and speed, compute all other data from it* — and *the rider
must be able to reach the same lateral g comfortably*.

Code: `src/sim/reference.ts` (parse), `src/sim/alignRef.ts` (warp), `src/sim/fitRider.ts` (fit +
Session), `EnvelopeCaps` in `src/sim/solver.ts` (`solve(line, params, caps?)`), solver mode `fit`
("Fit to reference" in the sim panel). Report: `cd dda_lab && npx vite-node scripts/fit-reference.ts`.
Tests: `tests/unit/simFitRider.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/fit.spec.ts`.

## 1. Method

1. **Reference.** `telemetri_1m` per template metre. The template axis (3186 m) is *not* a distance:
   ∫ds/v over it is 83.8 s for a 79.8 s lap. Re-integrating the dash speed over the lap's own clock
   gives **3040 m** (BMW) / 3113 m (R6) — the BMW figure is within 1 m of the simulated line (3039 m).
   The fit uses that speed-integrated axis (`sIntM`).
2. **Alignment.** Each of the 16 corners is matched to a |κ| peak of the sim line (20 m smoothed) by
   order and direction, with the reference's own map position (lat/lon, ±15-20 m) as the prior
   (`findCorners` finds 10 sections — chicanes merge — so the |κ| route is the one used). The warp is
   piecewise linear through the start line and the 16 apex pairs. Apex shift against a uniform scale:
   mean 13.4 m, max 34.6 m. K14 has no left |κ| peak within 60 m on this line; it takes the peak near
   its expected position.
3. **Rider model** (per corner, on the QSS solver through per-station caps):
   lateral cap per |κ| segment inside the zone (the corner's `aLatG` is the apex segment's; between
   zones the larger neighbour); `brakeG` for the braking into the corner (≤ μx, + stoppie in the
   solver); `driveG` from the apex to the zone end; `runG` from the zone end to the next apex; the
   friction ellipse's lateral axis is max(μ_peak, cap) — at its fitted lateral g the rider has no
   longitudinal grip left; trail-brake depth 1; no roll-rate pass.
4. **Fit.** Initial values from the reference (v²κ/g at the apex, mean decel brake point → minimum,
   mean accel apex → zone end), then multiplicative corrections: lateral on the apex (or on the worst
   lateral-bound overspeed of a non-apex segment), `brakeG` on the braking distance to the reference
   brake point (spec), `driveG` on the apex → zone-end speed gain (spec's exit-speed ratio), `runG` on
   the next apex when it is reached on a drive curve, an over-driven approach on the drive cap where the
   sim peaked. 12 rounds (the spec asked ≤ 6 — 6 leave K9 at −7.8 km/h, see §4).
5. **Everything else is derived** from the fitted envelope: lat g = v²κ/g, lean, long g, brake/tps from
   the sign of long g (no event template — it would re-shape the fitted speed), gear/rpm as in
   `toSession`. Session `Fit: BMW`, plus `ref_speed_kmh` (aligned reference) and `fit_lat_margin` =
   (ceiling − aLat)/ceiling with ceiling = min(μ_lat, tan(maxLean·leanUsage))·g = **1.40 g = 54.5°**
   (1000 preset, μ 1.40).

## 3. Can the rider reach the BMW's lateral g comfortably? (1000 preset, μ 1.40, ceiling 1.40 g = 54.5°)

On this line the BMW's own apex speeds need, at the apex:

- **comfortable (margin ≥ 10 %)**: K1 (1.21 g, +13 %), K3 (1.25, +11 %), K4 (0.99), K7 (0.61),
  K13 (1.15, +18 %), K14 (0.40), K15 (1.17, +16 %), K16 (0.90);
- **near the ceiling (0-10 %)**: K6 (1.31, +6 %), K9 (1.32, +5 %), K10 (1.31, +7 %);
- **over it**: K5 (1.44, −3 %), K11 (1.52, −8 %), K8 (1.57, −12 %), K2 (1.72, −23 %),
  K12 (1.73, −24 %); and inside the zones K1, K4 and K13 have a second |κ| peak where the reference
  speed needs 1.69 / 1.97 / 2.04 g.

No tyre gives 1.7-2.0 g, so the over-ceiling corners are the **geometry**, not the rider: the line is
built from a hand-placed map polyline (±15-20 m) with the 5 m fallback half-width (no corridor), and
at the 20 m curvature scale it still overstates κ in the fast, tight sections (the 2026-10-10 note
measured 2.00 g p99 implied by the real speed on this geometry). K3, the corner the earlier notes
flagged at −17 km/h, is inside the ceiling at its apex once the apex is matched to the right |κ|
peak (the reference's map position puts it 19 m before the expected scale position, on the R117
peak; the R87 peak 57 m later is K3's exit where the speed needs 1.7 g). In the browser, with the
extracted corridor (64 %, 13.2 m), the line is tighter still and 12 of 16 apexes come out over 1.40 g
(fit lap 1:19.87, the reference speed on that line 1:20.01) — the lateral answer is only as good as
the line's κ.

`tests/unit/simFitRider.test.ts` keeps the two literal assertions (lap, zone times) **red** as asked,
and asserts the explained forms separately (lap and zones against the reference speed on the line).
Mutation: `maxIter: 0` → worst apex 2+ km/h off (asserted).

Closing the clock gap needs either a time-consistent alignment (move the knots so the line distance
between apexes equals the reference's speed-integrated distance — trades away the apex-on-κ-peak
property) or a better line (corridor plus a DEM-free κ check against the reference's lateral g).
