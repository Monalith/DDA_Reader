# BMW 1:19.81: re-reading K8 and K12 from the onboard video (2026-10-11)

The question: the BMW reference (`_inbox/serres_BMW_1-19-81.json`) asks for 1.5–1.8 g at K8 and K12.
`2026-10-11-ducati-lateral-g.md` §4–5 and `2026-10-11-bmw-fit-pist-line.md` §6.3 call that
implausible. Are the file's speeds wrong there, or is the bike in a different place?

**Short answer: the speeds are mostly right. The apex positions are wrong.** At both corners the file
takes the "apex" speed from a point where the bike has already left the inside kerb and is accelerating.

- **K8.** The bike is on the inside kerb at **112–114 km/h**, not at 122.9. The file's K8 trace is also
  interpolated across a 2.6 s gap, and it reads +2–4 km/h high over most of that gap (34.5–35.0 s and 35.4–35.9 s).
- **K12.** Every dash reading in the file is correct, to ±1 km/h. But the bike is on the K12 inside
  kerb at t ≈ 55.2–55.9 s (**102–114 km/h, mid-kerb 107**). The file's apex is at t 56.50 s, 124 km/h:
  **~1.0 s and ~35 m too late**.
- **Controls.** K5 and K10 confirm the file exactly, both in speed and in when the minimum happens.

With the corrected apex speeds, the race κ 0 line needs about **1.25 g at K8 and 1.23–1.30 g at K12**,
against 1.48 / 1.66 g before. Both are now under the 1.40 g ceiling.

## 2. Method

1. **Frames.** Frames were extracted with ffmpeg (`select=between(n,a,b)`, passthrough timing) over
   ±3 s around each file apex time.
   - K5: frames 666–816
   - K8: frames 918–1068
   - K10: frames 1119–1269
   - K11→K12: frames 1330–1584
2. **Crops.** The dash area (150×70 px at 205,185) was cropped, upscaled ×2 and tiled with the frame
   number and t on each tile.
   - **How often.** Every 3rd frame (0.12 s), and every frame through the K8 gap (948–1003).
   - **How read.** By eye, on the Alpha Racing speed field.
3. **Position.** Full-frame sheets show where the inside kerb is. On this camera, the inside kerb fills
   the upper inside quadrant when the bike is at the apex.
4. **Distance.** Times are converted to the file's own `s_m` through `ham_veri`. Corner geometry comes
   from `_inbox/serres.pist.json`.
5. **Lean.** I tried to measure lean from horizon roll and dropped it. At apex lean the fairing hides
   the horizon, and the fit locked onto the fairing edge, so there is no lean-based g here.

The glare at K8 (frames 950–995) washes the digits out, which is why the automatic OCR left that 2.6 s
gap. The digits are still readable by eye on every frame. Where a frame is uncertain it is marked "?".

## 5. Lateral g at the corrected speeds (race κ 0)

The base is the race κ 0 apex lateral g from `2026-10-11-bmw-fit-pist-line.md`: §6.3 for K8 and K12,
§2 for K5 and K10. It is scaled by **(v / v_ref)²** with the line radius unchanged. This assumes the κ 0
line's apex radius applies at the kerb, which is where the bike actually is.

| corner | v_ref | g at v_ref, κ 0 kerb 1.0 / kerb 0 | corrected v | g at corrected v, kerb 1.0 / kerb 0 | lean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K5 | 70.1 | — / 0.92 | 70 | — / 0.92 | 43° |
| K10 | 79.2 | — / 1.04 | 79 | — / 1.03 | 46° |
| **K8** | 122.9 | 1.48 / 1.60 | **113** (112–114) | **1.25 / 1.35** (1.23–1.28 / 1.33–1.38) | 51–54° |
| **K12** | 124.2 | 1.66 / 1.69 | **107** (mid-kerb; 102–114 across the kerb) | **1.23 / 1.25** (1.12–1.40 / 1.14–1.42) | 51° (48–55°) |

- **Both corners drop under the 1.40 g ceiling** at the speed the bike carries on the kerb.
- **K12 at kerb end.** Taking K12 at the kerb's end (114) gives 1.40 g, at the limit. That is the
  conservative bound.
- **Agreement with the earlier shift test.** It also matches the earlier "±20 m shift" result (K8
  1.26 g, K12 1.48 g): the error is position, and a 20 m shift was not enough at K12.

## 6. Proposed corrections (the reference JSON is not modified)

1. **`viraj_ozeti` K8.**
   - `apeks_m` 1393 → **1375** (pist apexS 1380).
   - `hiz_kmh.apeks` 122.9 → **113**.
   - `bolge_basi` stays at 118.8.
   - The minimum is 112 at t ≈ 35.1 s.
2. **`ham_veri` frames 950–1005.**
   - Replace the interpolated values with the per-frame readings of §3.
   - Main deltas:
     - 958–967: −3 to −5 km/h
     - 983–996: −2 to −3 km/h
   - Rerun the smoothing, and recompute `s_m` from 34 s onward.
   - The integral shrinks by ~2 m, which is negligible against ±15–20 m.
3. **`viraj_ozeti` K12.**
   - `apeks_m` 2117 → **~2085** (mid-kerb; kerb spans file s 2071–2095).
   - `hiz_kmh.apeks` 124.2 → **107**.
   - The bike's apex is ~55.5 s.
   - Alternatively keep `apeks_m` and add an explicit alignment point.
4. **Alignment anchors.**
   - **Add the kerb times as anchors in the distance alignment:**

     | anchor | t s |
     |---|---|
     | K8 kerb | ~35.1 |
     | K11 turn-in kerb | 51.3 |
     | K12 kerb | 55.5 |

   - **Drop K11's speed minimum as an anchor.** The K11 minimum (53.5 s) is mid-corner on a long right
     with no kerb, so it is a poor anchor.
   - **Match apex to apex.** Then the pist K12 apex (2116) can be matched to the kerb rather than to
     the exit.
5. **Line model (separate from the data).**
   - **What the pist does.** It splits the second right-hander (2050–2150) into K11-apex-2 (2071, R 31)
     and K12 (2116, R 36), with a left wiggle at 2090.
   - **What the video shows.** One inside kerb for that turn, so this looks like a centreline-trace
     artefact.
   - **Consequence.** Matching the reference to the 2116 peak will keep producing exit-speed
     "apexes".
