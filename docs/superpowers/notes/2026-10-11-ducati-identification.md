## Signal conditioning

- **GPS.** 18 % of consecutive raw fixes are repeats, so the GPS updates more slowly than the 10 Hz log and the export's per-sample `gps_speed_kmh` (and with it `wheel_slip_pct`) jumps by tens of km/h from sample to sample. Ground speed is therefore GPS distance over a window. The GPS lags the wheel speed by **0.16 s** (grid search on upright samples, residual 4.88 km/h, n 1178). The dash reads **6.7 % high** upright.
- **Slip lean correction (R2 §3.6).** Windowed slip (1.1 s) = c0 + c2·(1 − cos φ) on coasting samples. Strict selection (tps < 8 %, |a| < 0.05 g, no DTC): n 20, c0 7.22 %, c2 25.05 %, 1 − cos φ range 0.090–0.286. Relaxed (tps < 30 %, |a| < 0.08 g): n 1023, c0 4.52 %, c2 45.54 %, range 0.035–0.386, rms 3.33 %. **Used: relaxed.** The strict selection hardly ever sees lean: this rider holds maintenance throttle through the corners, so there is almost no closed-throttle sample at lean and c2 cannot be fitted from it. The relaxed selection keeps "no net acceleration" but allows maintenance throttle, where true slip is a fraction of a percent. The lean term at 45° is 13.3 %.
- **Ground speed and g.** v = wheel / (1 + baseline(φ)). a_x = derivative of Savitzky-Golay (7 samples for peaks, 5 for the build, which is an upper bound per R3 §6.3). Tyre g = a_x + aero/rolling loss. Braking is measured from 0.3 s after the throttle closes. When the throttle snaps shut on the straight, the wheel speed drops by ~4 % within 0.2 s with no brake on (e.g. 256 → 245 km/h): that is drive slip collapsing, and it is the sign that the dash speed comes from the REAR wheel. A braking peak is the 10th percentile of the samples below −0.2 g, not the single deepest sample (median deepest/robust = 1.07). Cross-checks on braked zones: the GPS speed drop over the zone / the corrected wheel speed drop = 0.98; the export's `accel_long_g` minimum (raw wheel derivative, dash over-read and wheel hop included) / the robust corrected total peak = 1.30.
- **What is not a sensor.** `accel_lat_g` is tan(lean), not an accelerometer, so R3 §6.3 (b)'s lean-vs-lateral-g check cannot be done with this file. `wheelie` is a heuristic flag from dda_core (tps > 75, a > 0.45 g, gear ≤ 3, |lean| < 12°). It is used only to drop roll-ons. Ducati does not document which wheel `speed_kmh` comes from. The ~4 % drop at throttle closure points to the rear, which is the wheel R_d needs. Treat that as an inference, not a specification. The DTC "fast" torque reductions are single 0.1 s samples at maintenance throttle (tps 10-20 %) and 35-50° lean, about five per lap. None of them coincides with a gear change, so they are not quickshifter cuts.
