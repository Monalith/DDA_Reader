Frequently asked questions

Is MotoRaceLaps / DDA Lab free?

Yes. The hosted app at motoracelaps.com/lab is free to use with a Google or e-mail sign-in; the code is open source (MIT) and can also run on your own computer.

Are my lap files uploaded?

No. Files are decoded and analysed inside your browser. Only track models, schema images and corridors you choose to save are stored on the server.

Which files can I open?

Ducati DDA / DDA+ GPS .dda files, DDA_Reader .json and .csv exports, RaceChrono and onboard-video telemetry JSON, and DDA Lab .lab.json bundles.

How are laps detected?

From the GPS trace crossing the start/finish gate of the track model (built from your own GPS), or from the DDA lap beacon. You can move the start line on the map and laps are recomputed.

Can I compare laps from different days or bikes?

Yes. Open several files, tick laps from any of them, pick one as the reference. Laps align by distance along the track, so Δ time and Δ speed work across sessions.

What is the virtual rider?

A lap-time simulator: machine, tyre and rider sliders produce a simulated lap on your track that behaves like a recorded one, so you can see what braking 0.1 g harder or using 2° more lean would be worth.

Does it work for other tracks than Serres?

Yes. The track model is built from your GPS data for any circuit; Serres is just the built-in example.

Can I use it on a phone?

The app is designed for a laptop or tablet screen; the landing pages work on phones.
