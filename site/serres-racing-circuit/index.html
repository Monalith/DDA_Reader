Serres Racing Circuit — learn the track from real lap data

Serres Racing Circuit sits near Serres in northern Greece: 3.186 km, 16 turns, a long main straight into a heavy braking zone at K1 and a flowing, technical middle sector. It is one of the most popular track-day venues for riders from Türkiye, Bulgaria and Greece. This guide is built from three complete laps that ship inside DDA Lab — open them and follow along on the satellite map.

Turn by turn

Turn names follow the circuit map (K1–K16). Distances are from the start/finish line; speeds are from the 1000 cc reference lap unless stated.

K1 — the big stop

The main straight ends in the hardest braking of the lap: from about 270 km/h down to the low 100s. The fast riders brake past the 150 m board and trail the brake to the apex; the intermediate lap brakes roughly 40 m earlier and arrives 15 km/h slower at the turn-in point. Compare the long_g traces: a 1000 cc rider holds around −1.1 g, the intermediate about −0.8 g.

K2–K3 — direction changes

Two medium-speed turns where the 600 gains: carrying 5–8 km/h more mid-corner costs nothing on exit because the next straight is short. Watch lean and d_speed against the reference lap.

K4–K6 — the flowing sector

Linked corners where line matters more than power. The corner-speed style (wide in, long arc) of the R6 is visible on the map as a rounder trace; the S1000RR squares the exits off and leans on acceleration.

K7–K8 — the chicane

Quick left-right; the time is made by minimising the time at low speed. Markers on the two apexes make the comparison one click: pin them in DDA Lab and read the fixed values under each chart.

K9–K10 — second braking zone

K10 is the second heaviest stop. Riders who brake too early here lose almost as much as at K1, because the exit leads onto a long acceleration zone.

K11–K13 — technical middle

Slow, tight radii; gear choice and throttle pick-up point dominate. The Δ time chart usually shows the intermediate lap bleeding 0.3–0.5 s per corner here.

K14–K16 — onto the straight

The final sequence sets the straight-line speed for the whole run to K1. A clean exit from K16 is worth more than any single apex on the lap: a 5 km/h exit deficit becomes roughly 0.3 s by the next braking point.

How to use these laps for your own riding

  1. Open DDA Lab, pick the lap closest to your bike class as the reference (◉).
  2. Open your own Ducati DDA / RaceChrono / onboard-video file next to it.
  3. Switch to the Δ vs reference template: green where you gain, red where you lose, turn by turn.
  4. Use Focus on one turn, place markers at your braking point and apex, and compare speed, lean and throttle.
